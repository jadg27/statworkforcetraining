02 / The Service

Managed Compliance

We're not a course mill, and we're not the trainer. STAT Workforce Training is the service that connects your crew to authorized providers and keeps their training current — so you're never the contractor holding up a job because someone's card lapsed.

01

We log every card

Each worker's OSHA, CPR, forklift and specialty cards tracked in one roster — by name, trade, and expiration date.

02

We watch the renewals

Before a card lapses, we flag it and line up the refresher. Audit day never catches your crew short.

03

You book through us

Pay up front, we reserve the seat per order. No bulk seat purchases, no inventory sitting on your books.

04

We arrange the hands-on part

Forklift practicals, workplace evaluations, and in-person site safety arranged locally across Nassau, Suffolk, and the NY metro through our authorized partners. CPR runs through a partner AHA Training Center.
