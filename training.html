How to read this page. STAT Workforce Training is a referral & booking service. Courses marked Booking available can be scheduled now through our authorized training partners. Courses marked On request or Planned are coordinated case-by-case or are not yet active. STAT Workforce Training does not issue certifications or provide the instruction itself — all cards are issued by the authorized provider.
