What these services are — and aren't. These are administrative training-management services: organizing records, coordinating booking, and tracking renewals. They are not legal compliance certification and not a replacement for a qualified site safety professional. STAT Workforce Training does not issue certifications or provide the training itself; courses and cards come from authorized third-party providers.
