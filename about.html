STAT Workforce Training exists to take the administrative weight of safety training off a contractor's plate — booking the right courses, keeping records straight, and making sure no card slips through the cracks.

Who we are

A referral & booking service built around the jobsite

Construction companies juggle dozens of certifications across crews and jobsites — OSHA 10 and 30, NYC SST, CPR, forklift, and more. Keeping all of it current, documented, and ready for a GC or an owner is a job in itself.

STAT Workforce Training handles that work. We connect your team with authorized training providers, coordinate scheduling, and organize the records so your workforce stays jobsite-ready — across New York and Florida.

We're clear about our role: we're a safety-training referral & booking service and administrative training-record provider. We don't teach the courses or issue the cards — authorized third-party providers do that. What we do is make the whole process organized and painless.

OUR ROLE

What STAT Workforce Training does

  • Refers & books training with authorized providers
  • Organizes and tracks training records
  • Coordinates group & on-site sessions
OUR ROLE

What we don't do

  • Issue certifications or OSHA cards
  • Provide the instruction ourselves
  • Replace a qualified site safety professional
